How to play
  • Each player starts with 10 coins. Yellow costs 1, Red costs 3, Blue costs 6 — paid from your bank when you place a piece. Can't afford it, can't select it.
  • Click a tray piece, then click a highlighted hex in your front row to place it (0 or 1 per turn).
  • Click a piece on the board (up to 2 per turn) to move it. In a stack, clicking a piece moves it and everything stacked above it — hover to preview what will move.
  • Yellow moves 1, Red moves 2, Blue moves 3 hexes. The bottom piece being moved sets the range.
  • For multi-space moves, hover to trace the exact route (a black line trails behind you) — hop through opponents or coins along the way, not just at the end. Hover back to backtrack; going somewhere the trace can't simply reach snaps to a fresh route there.
  • Hold Ctrl (or Cmd) while tracing to let a route double back over itself — e.g. step out onto a coin and return home, or strike an opponent and retreat. Each step gets numbered so you can read the order, and you can finish back on the hex you started from.
  • Landing on an opponent's hex captures their whole stack there. Landing on your own stacks (any color mix, max 3).
  • Use the buttons at the top right to start a 2-player game (Circles vs Squares, on opposite edges) or a 3-player game. Switching mid-game asks first, and Undo will bring the abandoned game back.
  • The star token shows the coin-drop clock. 6 coins rain onto empty tiles at the start of any turn where the active player holds a yellow star — that's every 4 turns with 3 players, every 3 turns with 2.
  • Land pieces on a tile holding coins to collect them into your bank.
  • Click the round robot button beside a player to hand them to the computer. It cycles off (human) → blue (easy) → yellow (medium) → red (hard) → off.
  • Press Esc or click your tray to put a held piece back. Use Undo to step back through moves.
  • First to place all Yellow, Red & Blue pieces wins.
